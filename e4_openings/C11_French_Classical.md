**5. f4** is close to automatic (90.1% of masters games), reinforcing e5 before Black can undermine it. **5. Qg4**, the *Gledhill Attack*, is a real minority (0.2% masters) — Stockfish actually rates it slightly better for Black (−0.5), an aggressive try that isn't objectively rewarded.

**5. f4 c5 6. dxc5** reaches a genuine, if narrow-sample (62 masters games total), fork: **6... Bxc5** (51.6%) heads for **7. Qg4**, the *Bradford Attack* (dead level per Stockfish, +0.0), while **6... Nc6** (46.8%, stays C11, unnamed) heads toward **7. a3 Bxc5 8. Qg4 O-O 9. Nf3 f6**, the *Brodsky-Jones Variation* — one of the deepest single lines in this whole card, not built out further here. **6. Nf3** (instead of 6. dxc5) **Nc6 7. Be3** reaches the *Boleslavsky Variation* (+0.2, a modest White edge). None of these narrow-sample branches carry their own further ECO code — all stay C11.
