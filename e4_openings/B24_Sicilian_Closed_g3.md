> [!NOTE]
> **4. Bg2 Bg7 5. d3 e6 6. Be3 Nd4 7. Nce2!?**, the *Smyslov Variation* (+0.2, mention-only), keeps ... d6/... e6 flexible and offers a trade of knights on d4/e2 rather than the more common ... d6 tabiya — a real, if secondary, try (133 masters games). Masters split between **7... Ne7** (45.9%) and **7... Nxe2** (18.8%).
