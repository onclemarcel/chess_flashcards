# B00 King's Pawn Game <br> 1. e4 #

**1. e4**, the King's Pawn Opening, is the most popular first move at all levels of the game. It opens lines to develop the queen and the king's bishop, fights for control of the centre, and moves White one step closer to kingside castling. Its only drawback is that the e4 pawn is left undefended.

### Candidate moves

* [**1... c5**](https://github.com/onclemarcel/chess_flashcards/blob/main/e4_openings/B20_Sicilian.md) (+0.3): the [Sicilian Defense](https://github.com/onclemarcel/chess_flashcards/blob/main/e4_openings/B20_Sicilian.md), the single most popular reply at master level (45.9%) — Black fights for the centre asymmetrically rather than matching White pawn for pawn
* [**1... e5**](https://github.com/onclemarcel/chess_flashcards/blob/main/e4_openings/C20_KPG.md) (+0.2): the classical reply — Black grabs an equal share of the centre and controls d4, at 24.0% of masters games
* [**1... e6**](https://github.com/onclemarcel/chess_flashcards/blob/main/e4_openings/C00_French_Defense.md) (+0.2): the [French Defense](https://github.com/onclemarcel/chess_flashcards/blob/main/e4_openings/C00_French_Defense.md) — Black prepares ... d5 before playing it, at the cost of shutting in the light-squared bishop for a while (11.7% masters)
* [**1... d5**](https://github.com/onclemarcel/chess_flashcards/blob/main/e4_openings/B01_Scandinavian.md) (+0.5): the [Scandinavian Defense](https://github.com/onclemarcel/chess_flashcards/blob/main/e4_openings/B01_Scandinavian.md), Black challenges e4 immediately and forces White to make an early decision on the d5 pawn
* [**1... c6**](https://github.com/onclemarcel/chess_flashcards/blob/main/e4_openings/B10_Caro_Kann.md) (+0.3): the [Caro-Kann Defense](https://github.com/onclemarcel/chess_flashcards/blob/main/e4_openings/B10_Caro_Kann.md) — the same ... d5 preparation as the French, but keeping the light-squared bishop free to develop first (8.1% masters)
* [**1... d6**](https://github.com/onclemarcel/chess_flashcards/blob/main/e4_openings/B07_Pirc_Defense.md) (+0.4): the [Pirc Defense](https://github.com/onclemarcel/chess_flashcards/blob/main/e4_openings/B07_Pirc_Defense.md) — Black delays the centre entirely, planning ... Nf6/... g6/... Bg7 before deciding how to react (3.4% masters)
* [**1... g6**](https://github.com/onclemarcel/chess_flashcards/blob/main/e4_openings/B06_Modern_Defense.md) (+0.6): the [Modern Defense](https://github.com/onclemarcel/chess_flashcards/blob/main/e4_openings/B06_Modern_Defense.md) — the same hypermodern idea as the Pirc, reached by fianchettoing first (2.7% masters)
* [**1... Nf6**](https://github.com/onclemarcel/chess_flashcards/blob/main/e4_openings/B02_Alekhine_Defense.md) (+0.7): the [Alekhine Defense](https://github.com/onclemarcel/chess_flashcards/blob/main/e4_openings/B02_Alekhine_Defense.md) — provokes White's centre forward instead of contesting it, rare (1.6% masters) and objectively a touch worse for Black, but a real practical trap against an unprepared opponent
* [**1... Nc6**](https://github.com/onclemarcel/chess_flashcards/blob/main/e4_openings/B00_Nimzowitsch_Defense.md) (+0.4): the [Nimzowitsch Defense](https://github.com/onclemarcel/chess_flashcards/blob/main/e4_openings/B00_Nimzowitsch_Defense.md) — develops a piece before committing any pawn structure, rare (0.5% masters) but fully playable
