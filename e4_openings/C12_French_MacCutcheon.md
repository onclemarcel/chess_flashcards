**6. Bd2** is masters' clear main try (63.6%), retreating the bishop while offering it up for the knight on c3 later. Every other 6th-move try is its own named line, none built out further here:

* **6. exf6** (4.6%): the *Chigorin Variation* — captures at once rather than retreating.
* **6. Be3** (18.1%): the *Janowski Variation*.
* **6. Bh4** (3.3%): the *Bernstein Variation*.
* **6. Bc1** (10.2%): the *Dr. Olland (Dutch) Variation*.

**6. Bd2 Bxc3** is masters' overwhelming choice at that fork (98.9%!) — a genuine finding: the *Lasker Variation* (the capture) is played almost every time in practice, while **6... Nfd7**, the line `eco.md` lists first and calls the *Tartakower Variation*, is in fact the rare exception (0.9% masters). **7. bxc3 Ne4** reaches a further fork: **8. Qg4 Kf8 9. Bc1** is the *Duras Variation*, and **8. Qg4 g6** is a separate named try (the *Lasker Variation, 8...g6*) — both real database rarities at this depth (12 masters games total at the 8. Qg4 fork), not built out further here.
