# C68 Ruy Lopez: Exchange Variation <br> 1. e4 e5 2. Nf3 Nc6 3. Bb5 a6 4. Bxc6 #

Spun off from [C60's own "3... a6" section](https://github.com/onclemarcel/chess_flashcards/blob/main/e4_openings/C60_Ruy_Lopez.md#_a6_) — masters' clear second choice at that fork (8.8%, far more popular online at 24.5%), already live-tagged its own code at this exact bare ply (before Black even recaptures). Trades the bishop for the knight outright, giving Black doubled c-pawns in exchange for the bishop pair — a simpler, more forcing structure that's easier to play without deep preparation, and a longtime World Champion favourite (Fischer, among others, used it to great effect).
