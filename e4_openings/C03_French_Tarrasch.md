# C03 French Defense: Tarrasch Variation <br> 1. e4 e6 2. d4 d5 3. Nd2 #

Spun off from [`C00_French_Defense.md`](https://github.com/onclemarcel/chess_flashcards/blob/main/e4_openings/C00_French_Defense.md)'s own candidate list — masters' second-most-popular try there (31.3%). Sidesteps the pin that 3... Bb4 would otherwise place on a knight developed to c3, at the cost of blocking the c1-bishop's most natural diagonal for a while. Named for Siegbert Tarrasch, who championed it as a calmer alternative to 3. Nc3.
