Black has a genuine near-even fork: **7... Qc7** (32.2% masters, the line that actually leads toward the "poisoned pawn" ideas below) and **7... O-O** (42.1%, a real practical alternative avoiding the whole complex).

**7... Qc7 8. Qxg7 Rg8 9. Qxh7 cxd4 10. Kd1** is the *Euwe-Gligoric Variation* — genuinely dead level per Stockfish (+0.0) despite White having grabbed two pawns, since Black's own attack down the g-file and against the exposed king is real compensation. **10. Ne2** (instead of 10. Kd1) is the *Konstantinopolsky Variation*, a modest real White edge (+0.6). Both are deep, narrow-sample lines (well under 100 masters games at this exact depth) at the very heart of the French's own most heavily analysed theoretical dispute, not built out further here.
