<a name="_Wurzburger_"></a>

> [!NOTE]
> **5. d3 Qh4+ 6. g3 Nxg3 7. Nf3 Qh5 8. Nxd5** is the *Wurzburger trap* — Black's knight grabs a second pawn on g3 before the queen retreats, but White's own knight lands on d5 with real threats. Despite the "trap" name, the resulting position is genuinely dead level per Stockfish (+0.0) — not an outright refutation of Black's greed, just a fair fight.
