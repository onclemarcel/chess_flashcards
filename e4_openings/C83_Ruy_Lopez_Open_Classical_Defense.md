**11... Qd7?** looks natural — bringing the queen toward the kingside and preparing ... Rad8 — but it walks into **12. Nxe6 fxe6 13. Rxe4!**, netting White a full extra piece: the rook simply takes the knight, and Black has nothing adequate to answer with (recapturing on e4 does not work out favourably here). A genuine, massive engine swing (+3.46) confirming this is a real, punishing practical trap, not just a slightly worse continuation — at the position right after the rook lands on e4, players online still reach for **... Bc5** far more than the better **... dxe4** (85.7% vs 14.3%), suggesting the trap continues to claim victims in casual play. Not built out further here beyond this trap summary (backlog).
