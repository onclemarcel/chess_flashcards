# 1. e4 d5 : The Scandinavian Defense (B01) #

Black move directly brings White to take a decision: take or leave the d5 pawn:\
\
**Case 1**  - White leaves the pawn:

> [!NOTE]
> - If **1. d3**, then **... dxe4 2. dxe4** leaves the d-column open, Black may now exchange the Queens with White King exposed on d1
>
>    ![](../e4_openings/pics/e4_d5_2_d3.png)<br>
>     Scandinavian 1. e4 d5 2. d3 <br>
>     *Lichess: Very Rare (0%)*<br>
>     *Stockfish -0.1*
>

> [!NOTE]
> - If **1. Nc3**, then **... d4** gives more space to Black while attacking the knight.
>     - **2. Nd5** is met by **... e5**, which blocks the escape squares of the knight on d5, then c6 picks up the White Knight
>
