Develops the queenside knight, adding a second defender to e4 rather than resolving the tension immediately — this exact position is independently live-tagged the ***Dyckhoff Variation*** too (a genuine second name for the same node, not a divergence from `eco.md`'s own *Berger Variation*, just an additional one). Masters' clear reply is **4... fxe4** (81.8%), grabbing the pawn back at once. Not built out further here (backlog).
