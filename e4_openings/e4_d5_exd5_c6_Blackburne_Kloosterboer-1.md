# 2... c6 : the Blackburne-Kloosterboer Gambit (B01)

*1. e4 d5 2. exd5 c6*

Black offers a second pawn to open lines for the minor pieces. Dubious, but sharp.

### 3... e5 — the Kloosterboer Gambit
