# C01 French Defense: Exchange Variation <br> 1. e4 e6 2. d4 d5 3. exd5 #

Spun off from [`C00_French_Defense.md`](https://github.com/onclemarcel/chess_flashcards/blob/main/e4_openings/C00_French_Defense.md)'s own candidate list — masters' least popular try at that fork (5.9%), far more common in casual play (29.3% online).
