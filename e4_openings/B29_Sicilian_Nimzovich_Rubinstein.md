Live-tagged the *Main Line* (`eco.md` calls this exact tabiya the "Rubinstein Counter-Gambit," a real name divergence). A forced-looking sequence: the knight gets kicked to d5, trades itself off on c3, and Black recaptures toward an isolated-queen's-pawn-style structure with an extra tempo for development. **7. dxc5** is masters' overwhelming reply (81.6%), grabbing the pawn since Black's own d5 pawn is already isolated. Deeper Nimzowitsch Variation theory past this point is its own extensive body of work, not covered further here — this is the natural end of the whole B20-B29 Sicilian sidelines sweep as covered across this batch's card set.
