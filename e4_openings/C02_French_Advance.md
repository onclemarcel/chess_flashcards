* [**4. c3**](#_c3_) (95.1% masters): masters' overwhelming main try — see below.
* **4. Nf3** (2.9% masters): the *Nimzovich System* — stays C02, not built out further here.

* **4. dxc5** (0.7% masters): the *Steinitz Variation* — releases the central tension early (−0.3), stays C02, not built out further here.
