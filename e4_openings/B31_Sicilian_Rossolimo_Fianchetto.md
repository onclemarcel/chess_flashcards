# B31 Sicilian Defense, Rossolimo Variation: Fianchetto <br> 1. e4 c5 2. Nf3 Nc6 3. Bb5 g6 #

Spun off from [`B30_Sicilian_Nc6_Open.md`](https://github.com/onclemarcel/chess_flashcards/blob/main/e4_openings/B30_Sicilian_Nc6_Open.md)'s own **3. Bb5** section — **3... g6** (50.0% of masters replies to Bb5) is masters' single most popular answer to the Rossolimo check, live-tagged **B31** the moment it's played (Lichess calls the whole B30-B31 family the *Nyezhmetdinov-Rossolimo Attack*, not the more familiar *Nimzovich-Rossolimo* name `eco.md` uses — a real name divergence). Black fianchettoes to meet a future Bxc6 recapture with the bishop rather than a pawn, keeping the structure intact.

### Candidate moves

* **4. O-O** — masters' overwhelming main try, castling and preparing Re1 before deciding how to meet Black's bishop on g7.
* **4. Bxc6** — trades immediately for the doubled pawns, giving up the two bishops for a structural edge.
