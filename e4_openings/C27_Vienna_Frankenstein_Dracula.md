**4. Qh5** is close to automatic (94.4% of masters games), attacking both the e5 pawn and (once the knight moves) f7. **4. Nf3** is a genuine curiosity: it transposes straight into Petrov's Defense territory move-order-wise (matching `eco.md`'s own "Petrov's Defence" label here), yet the live explorer still tags it **C27** — just under a completely different name, *Boden-Kieseritzky Gambit* (borrowed from the Bishop's Opening family) rather than anything Petrov-flavoured. Stockfish rates it a real Black edge (−0.5). **4. Nf3 d5** is the further-named *Lichtenhein Defense* (+0.2). Neither built out further here.

## 5. Bb3

[![5. Bb3](https://backscattering.de/web-boardimage/board.svg?fen=rnbqkb1r/pppp1ppp/3n4/4p2Q/8/1BN5/PPPP1PPP/R1B1K1NR&lastMove=c4b3&coordinates=true&size=320)](https://lichess.org/analysis/standard/rnbqkb1r/pppp1ppp/3n4/4p2Q/8/1BN5/PPPP1PPP/R1B1K1NR_b_KQkq_-_3_5)

```
rnbqkb1r/pppp1ppp/3n4/4p2Q/8/1BN5/PPPP1PPP/R1B1K1NR b KQkq - 3 5
```

Black has two named continuations here — **5... Nc6**, heading for the *Frankenstein-Dracula Variation proper* (below), and **5... Be7 6. Nf3 Nc6 7. Nxe5**, the *Alekhine Variation* (a real Black edge per Stockfish, −0.2, dead level despite White's pawn grab, since Black's own development lead compensates — not built out further here).

**6... g6** is masters' and online's overwhelming reply (92.5%+), blocking the queen's own attack on e5 while preparing to meet the coming knight fork calmly. **6. d4** instead (rather than 6. Nb5) is the further-named *Adams' Gambit* — a real database rarity (8 masters games), Stockfish already rating it a real edge for Black (−0.7), an example of a "Gambit" name that doesn't reflect objective soundness.

**7. Qf3 f5 8. Qd5 Qe7 9. Nxc7+ Kd8 10. Nxa8 b6** is the *Frankenstein-Dracula Variation* proper (+0.6 per Stockfish) — White grabs the exchange (knight for rook) while Black's own pieces swarm the queenside and kingside for compensation, one of the most tactically dense tabiyas in the whole Vienna Game. Deeper theory not covered further here.
