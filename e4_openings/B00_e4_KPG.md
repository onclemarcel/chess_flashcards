# B00 King's Pawn Game <br> 1. e4 #

**1. e4**, the King's Pawn Opening, is the most popular first move at all levels of the game. It opens lines to develop the queen and the king's bishop, fights for control of the centre, and moves White one step closer to kingside castling. Its only drawback is that the e4 pawn is left undefended.

> [!NOTE]
> The Sicilian Defense (**1... c5**) is in fact the single most popular reply in masters play (45.9%) and edges out 1... e5 online too (25.3% vs 23.4%), but it doesn't yet have a dedicated card in this repository. The French (**1... e6**, 11.7% masters) and Caro-Kann (**1... c6**, 8.1% masters) pages already exist as stubs and are also pending conversion.
