# 2... Nf6 : the Modern Variation (B01)

*1. e4 d5 2. exd5 Nf6*

Black attacks the d5 pawn with a piece instead of the Queen, avoiding the loss of tempo of the Mieses-Kotroc.

* [**3. Nf3**](#_Nf3_) : quiet development, returning the pawn.
