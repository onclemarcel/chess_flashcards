# C44 Ponziani Opening <br> 1. e4 e5 2. Nf3 Nc6 3. c3 #

Nc6 hub](https://github.com/onclemarcel/chess_flashcards/blob/main/e4_openings/C44_Nc6_King_Knight.md), where c3 already sat in the stats table (0.6% masters, 3.0% online) with no candidate bullet or card pointing to it — a genuine zero-coverage gap surfaced by a full A00-E99 ECO-code audit. Named after 18th-century Italian player Domenico Lorenzo Ponziani: rather than develop a bishop, White prepares an immediate **d4**, building a full pawn centre before Black's own knight development can pressure e4.

**4. Qa4** pins the c6-knight to defend e4 indirectly while eyeing a future Bb5 — masters' clear main try. Three named replies fork from here, none built out further (all backlog):
