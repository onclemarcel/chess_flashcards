> [!NOTE]
> Masters recapture immediately with **3. Qxd4** roughly three times out of five (59.8%); online, that drops to 22.3%, and the *Danish Gambit* (**3. c3**) becomes the single most popular try instead (43.3%) — offering a second pawn for an even faster development lead, a much sharper and more forcing choice that rewards preparation more than accuracy.

* [**3. Qxd4**](#_Qxd4_) (+0.1): the simple, principled recapture — masters' main line.
* [**3. c3**](#_c3_) (+0.3): the *Danish Gambit* — offering a second pawn (and often a third with 3... dxc3 4. Bc4) to open lines toward f7 before Black can finish developing.
* **3. Nf3** (25.3% masters): stays C21, not built out further here. **3. Nf3 c5 4. Bc4 b5** is a real database rarity `eco.md` names the *Kieseritsky Variation* — a genuine gambit-like try that Stockfish already rates clearly worse for Black (+1.3), not built out further here.
* **3. f4** (1.2% masters): live-tagged the *Halasz-McDonnell Gambit* — a further name `eco.md`'s own "Halasz Gambit" doesn't fully carry, and Stockfish already rates it a real, above-average edge for *Black* (−1.3) — an example of a gambit that genuinely isn't rewarded. Not built out further here.

**A genuine online/masters inversion**: online, accepting with **3... dxc3** is by far the most common reply (58.9%). Masters instead mostly *decline* the gambit with **3... d5** (48.0%, the *Sørensen Defence* — dead level per Stockfish, +0.0) — accepting (28.5%) is only their second choice, and **3... Qe7** (the *Danish Gambit Declined*, 12.5%) is a real third try.

If Black does accept with **3... dxc3**, White typically continues **4. Bc4**, developing onto the same active diagonal seen in the Bishop's/Italian openings while offering yet another pawn (**4... cxb2 5. Bxb2**) for a huge lead in development and open lines against f7 — though at this full depth of acceptance, masters practice is extremely thin (20 games total). Black's own two named replies there are **5... d5**, the *Schlechter Defence* (dead level, +0.0), and **5... Qe7**, live-tagged the *Chigorin Defense* — a real name substitution for `eco.md`'s own "Collijn Defence" label (+0.6, a modest White edge). Well-prepared, Black can decline or return the material to reach a safe position; a Danish Gambit specialist can make this very dangerous over the board, which is why the engine's modest evaluation understates its practical bite.
