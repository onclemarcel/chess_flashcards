# C92 Ruy Lopez: Closed, Zaitsev Variation <br> 1. e4 e5 2. Nf3 Nc6 3. Bb5 a6 4. Ba4 Nf6 5. O-O Be7 6. Re1 b5 7. Bb3 d6 8. c3 O-O 9. h3 Bb7 #

Spun off from [C90's 9. h3](https://github.com/onclemarcel/chess_flashcards/blob/main/e4_openings/C90_Ruy_Lopez_Closed_Tabiya.md#_h3_): Black develops the bishop before committing the knight anywhere. The Lichess explorer itself tags this exact position "**C92 Ruy Lopez: Closed, Flohr System**" (named after Salo Flohr) — it only becomes the Zaitsev Variation proper one move later, after **... Re8**. Named for Soviet analyst Igor Zaitsev, this became Garry Kasparov's main defense against the Ruy Lopez for most of his career. Masters' third most common 9th-move try (24.1%).
