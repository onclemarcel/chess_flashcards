`eco.md` spells this the *Bogolyubov Variation*; the live explorer spells it ***Bogoljubow*** — the same 20th-century grandmaster, a real transliteration divergence. Masters' reply forks two ways: **10. Be3** (59.7%, defending d4 while eyeing a later Nbd2/Qd2 setup) and **10. d5** (37.8%, closing the centre at once, gaining space but releasing the tension).
