# Mate Patterns
<br>
This page lists recurring checkmate *patterns* — the recognisable final-position shapes and the tactical devices used to force them — with links to a deeper card for each one. It complements [Opening Traps](../traps/opening_traps.md), which indexes named *traps* in the opening rather than mating shapes reachable from any stage of the game.
<br>
<br>

Not every mate has a single, universally agreed name the way Anastasia's Mate or Smothered Mate do. Where a pattern doesn't cleanly match one of the classic named mates, its card describes the mechanism directly (which piece(s) deliver the final check, which piece(s) deny the escape squares, and what forces the king onto the mating square) rather than forcing an inaccurate label onto it.

### By mechanism

- **Corridor mates** — the king has no flight squares along a rank, file, or diagonal, usually because its own pieces block one side and an enemy piece covers the other.
  - [Corridor Mate (Queen + Long-Diagonal Bishop)](./Corridor_Mate/Corridor_Mate.md) — a queen delivers mate along an open file; a bishop already sitting on the long diagonal denies the only other escape square. Forced here by a clearance sacrifice that also produces a discovered double check along the way.
