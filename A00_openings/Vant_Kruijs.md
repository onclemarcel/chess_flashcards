# A00 Van't Kruijs Opening <br> 1. e3 #

Named after 19th-century Dutch player Maarten van't Kruijs. Frees the dark-squared bishop and prepares d4 without staking the full two-square central claim of 1. e4 — genuinely flexible rather than weak, often transposing into a French Defense or Queen's Pawn structure with colours or a tempo slightly altered. Stockfish rates it among the better tries on this whole page.
