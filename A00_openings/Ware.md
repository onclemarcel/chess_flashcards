# A00 Ware Opening <br> 1. a4 #

Also called the Meadow Hay Opening, after 19th-century American player Preston Ware, who played it repeatedly in tournament play despite its poor reputation. Grabs queenside space and prepares Ra3 as a way to swing the rook toward the kingside — a real idea, but one that does nothing for development or the centre on move one. Genuinely inferior to a normal first move, though not catastrophically so.
