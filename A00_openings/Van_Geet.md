# A00 Van Geet Opening <br> 1. Nc3 #

Also widely known as the Dunst Opening (after American master Theodore Dunst) — the explorer itself tags this exact position "Van Geet Opening", after Dutch correspondence player Dick van Geet, who championed it for decades, so that's the name used here. Develops a piece toward the centre immediately without committing any pawn structure at all, keeping a transposition into almost any other 1. e4/1. d4/1. c4 system on the table. Fully sound — practically dead equal — and a genuine flexible try rather than a trick.

### Candidate moves

* **1... d5** (45.2% masters): the clear main try, staking out the centre while White's knight has committed to c3 rather than a central pawn.
* **1... c5** (29.3% masters): a real second choice, often transposing toward Sicilian-like structures a tempo up for White (2. e4 reaching a normal Sicilian, or 2. d4 heading elsewhere).
