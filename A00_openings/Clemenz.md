# A00 Clemenz Opening <br> 1. h3 #

Named after the 19th-century Baltic-German player Paul Saladin Clemenz. The quietest possible first move: a single step by the rook pawn, ruling out ... Bg4/... Ng4 pins before they exist but otherwise committing to nothing. Not a serious try to fight for an edge — Stockfish already calls it dead equal — but completely sound, since Black gets nothing concrete to exploit either.

### Candidate moves

* **1... e5 / 1... d5**: Black simply takes the centre — White's h3 does nothing to contest either, so most games transpose straight into a normal 1. e4/1. d4 structure a tempo down for White (though that tempo is a fairly harmless one to have spent).
