# A00 Kádas Opening <br> 1. h4 #

Named after Hungarian player Gyula Kádas. Grabs kingside space and rules out ... Nf6-g4/... Bg4 ideas before they exist, but otherwise develops nothing and does nothing for the centre — a real cousin of Grob's Attack (1. g4) and the Ware Opening (1. a4) in spirit, all three spending the first move on flank space rather than development. Genuinely weak: Stockfish rates it close to half a pawn worse than a normal first move.
