# A00 Barnes Opening <br> 1. f3 #

Named after 19th-century English player Thomas Wilson Barnes — sometimes nicknamed the "Fried Fox" in club circles, especially when it walks into the trap below. Weakens the crucial e1-h4 diagonal in front of the king and blocks the king's knight's natural square, without any compensating benefit — genuinely one of the weakest first moves in the game. Stockfish already rates it worse than any other card in this A00 group except the Sodium Attack.
