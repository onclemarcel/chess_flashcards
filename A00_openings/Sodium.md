# A00 Sodium Attack <br> 1. Na3 #

Also called the Durkin Opening, after American player Cecil Durkin, who championed it. Like the Amar Opening (1. Nh3), the knight heads to the rim on move one — a3 covers only b5 and c4, and blocks the a-pawn's own advance. Genuinely weak: Stockfish rates it half a pawn worse than a normal developing move, with no real idea behind the choice of square.
