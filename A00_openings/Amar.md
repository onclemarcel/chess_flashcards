# A00 Amar Opening <br> 1. Nh3 #

Also called the Paris Opening. The knight heads to the rim on move one — a real violation of the "knights on the rim are dim" principle, since h3 covers only f4 and g5, neither especially useful this early. Genuinely weak rather than just unusual: Stockfish already rates it nearly half a pawn worse than a normal developing move, with no compensating idea behind the awkward square.

### Candidate moves

* **1... d5 / 1... e5**: Black just takes the full centre — with the knight already committed to a passive square, White has no good way to contest it.
