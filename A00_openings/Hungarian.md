# A00 Hungarian Opening <br> 1. g3 #

Often called Benko's Opening after Hungarian-American grandmaster Pal Benko — the explorer itself tags this exact position "Hungarian Opening", so that's the name used here, with Benko's noted as the common alternative. White fianchettoes the king's bishop before touching the centre at all, keeping every central structure (transposing toward the English, Réti, or a genuine King's Fianchetto system) available depending on Black's reply. Fully sound and flexible — the point isn't surprise value so much as move-order flexibility.
