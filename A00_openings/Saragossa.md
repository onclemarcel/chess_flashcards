# A00 Saragossa Opening <br> 1. c3 #

Named after the Spanish city where it was played in an early international correspondence event. Prepares d4 with the c-pawn already supporting it, while keeping the queen's route to a4 or b3 open — a modest but perfectly sound try, essentially a Slav or Caro-Kann structure played a tempo down for Black rather than White. Stockfish calls it dead equal.
