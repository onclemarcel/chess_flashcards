# A00 Mieses Opening <br> 1. d3 #

Named after German-British grandmaster Jacques Mieses. Prepares e4 (or a King's Indian Attack-style setup with e4/g3/Bg2/Nf3) without staking a claim on the centre with two squares at once — a genuinely flexible, sound try, effectively a King's Indian Attack a tempo behind where White normally reaches it via 1. e4 or 1. Nf3.

### Candidate moves

* **1... d5** (48.1% masters): by far the most common reply — Black takes the full centre while White has only prepared to contest it later.
* **1... e5 / 1... g6 / 1... c5**: all real minor tries, none close to d5's popularity.

> [!NOTE]
> Two named sub-lines, live-confirmed via [chessopenings.com](https://chessopenings.com/eco/A00): **1... e5 2. Nd2**, the **Valencia Opening**, develops the queen's knight toward e4/f3 rather than the more natural Nf3/g3 setup. **1... c5 2. Nc3 Nc6 3. g3**, the **Venezolana Opening**, adds a King's Indian Attack-style fianchetto against a Sicilian-like Black setup.
