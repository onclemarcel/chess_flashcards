# A00 Anderssen's Opening <br> 1. a3 #

Named after Adolf Anderssen, one of the strongest players of the mid-19th century (though he's far better remembered for his brilliancies with other openings than for this one). A genuine waiting move — it doesn't develop anything or fight for the centre, simply reserving a tempo and a retreat square for a future bishop, and inviting Black to commit first. Fully sound, if unambitious: Stockfish rates it barely worse than the main tries.

### Candidate moves

* **1... d5 / 1... e5**: Black simply takes the centre while White has spent the move on the flank — both real, both close in popularity (28.9% and 20.3% masters).
* **1... g6 / 1... Nf6**: also common (19.6% and 18.0% masters), keeping the position flexible rather than committing centrally right away.
