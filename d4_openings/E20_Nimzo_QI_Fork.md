# E20 Nimzo-Indian Defense <br> 1. d4 Nf6 2. c4 e6 #

Nc3** invites it; **3. Nf3** sidesteps it, at least for a move.

> [!NOTE]
> Masters barely favour **3. Nf3** (47.5%) over **3. Nc3** (40.1%) — but online it inverts hard, with Nc3 leading nearly 3:1 (65.1% vs 22.6%). Club players seem to prefer inviting the sharper Nimzo-Indian pin immediately, while masters are more willing to delay the decision.
