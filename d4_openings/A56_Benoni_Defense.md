# A56 Benoni Defense <br> 1. d4 Nf6 2. c4 c5 #

Its own **3. d5**, White's near-automatic reply (88.8% masters), forks hard into several tries — two of them (the Benko Gambit and Modern Benoni) already advance to their own dedicated codes/cards, while three real siblings stay genuinely A56-coded and live on this card.
