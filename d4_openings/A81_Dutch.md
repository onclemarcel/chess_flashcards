### Candidate moves

Back at move 3, before committing to the Semi-Leningrad's ... g6, Black has two other real tries:

* [**3... e6**](#_e6_) (+0.4, 21.2% masters): the Classical setup — Black develops the light-squared bishop before committing the kingside pawn structure, see below
* **3... d6** (6.0% masters): a slower move-order into similar Leningrad structures — not covered further here
* [**3... d5**](#_d5_) (0.9% masters): the Stonewall Dutch proper — a firm central pawn chain (d5/e6/f5) at the cost of the light-squared bishop's scope, see below
