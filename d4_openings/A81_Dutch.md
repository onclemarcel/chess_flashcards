### Candidate moves

* **3... e6** (+0.4, 21.2% masters): the Classical/Stonewall-adjacent setup — Black develops the light-squared bishop before committing the kingside pawn structure
* **3... d6** (6.0% masters): a slower move-order into similar Leningrad structures
* **3... d5** (0.9% masters): the Stonewall Dutch proper — a firm central pawn chain (d5/e6/f5) at the cost of the light-squared bishop's scope

None of these are built out yet in this repository (backlog).
