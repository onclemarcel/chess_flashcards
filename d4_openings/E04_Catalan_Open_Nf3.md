A wide, genuine spread of real tries at Black's own 5th move — no single dominant choice:

* **5... a6** (34.3% masters): masters' narrow plurality, preparing ... b5 to hold the extra pawn. Not built out further here (backlog).
* **5... Nc6** (20.9% masters): develops actively instead, eyeing d4/e5.
* **5... c5** (15.2% masters): strikes back at the centre immediately.
* **5... Bb4+** (13.2% masters): checks before completing development.
