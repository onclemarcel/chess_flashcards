# A80 Dutch Defense <br> 1. d4 f5 #

Live-confirmed the root of the whole Dutch Defense complex (A80-A99), cross-checked against [chessopenings.com's ECO reference](https://chessopenings.com/eco/A80). Black stakes out kingside space and eyes ... e5 ideas immediately, at the cost of weakening the e6/king's-diagonal light squares — the only fully committal reply to 1. d4 covered in this repository outside the symmetrical 1... d5/1... Nf6 main lines.
