Black's own 9th move is a genuine multi-way split, no dominant single try:

* **9... c5** (48.9% masters): masters' narrow plurality, striking at the centre before White can fully consolidate.
* **9... f5** (19.8% masters): stakes out kingside space instead.
* **9... d6** (15.4% masters): a quieter structural choice.
* **9... Be4** (9.7% masters): offers a further trade of pieces immediately.

Deeper Queen's Indian theory past this point is its own extensive body of work, not covered further here — this is the natural end of the whole E10-E19 sweep as covered across this card set.
