# E12 Queen's Indian Defense <br> 1. d4 Nf6 2. c4 e6 3. Nf3 b6 #

Where the Nimzo-Indian pins the knight, the Queen's Indian fianchettoes instead: Black prepares ... Bb7, eyeing the long diagonal and e4, while keeping ... Bb4+ or ... d5 in reserve for later. It is the natural companion to the Nimzo-Indian for players who want a solid, positional answer to 1. d4 regardless of whether White plays 3. Nc3 or 3. Nf3.
