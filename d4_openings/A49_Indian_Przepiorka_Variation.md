# A49 Indian Defense: Przepiorka Variation <br> 1. d4 Nf6 2. Nf3 g6 3. g3 #

Spun off from [A48_East_Indian_Defense.md](https://github.com/onclemarcel/chess_flashcards/blob/main/d4_openings/A48_East_Indian_Defense.md), where **3. g3** used to live as a candidate bullet. Live-confirmed via the Lichess explorer's own `opening` field — a real name divergence worth flagging: `eco.md` itself calls this line the generic **"Fianchetto Without c4"**, but Lichess live-tags it the ***Przepiorka Variation***, cross-checked against [chessopenings.com's ECO reference](https://chessopenings.com/eco/A49). White fianchettoes the king's bishop before committing the c-pawn at all — a King's-Indian-style setup with colours reversed in spirit, delaying c4 indefinitely.
