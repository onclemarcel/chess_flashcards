# A84 Dutch Defense <br> 1. d4 f5 2. c4 #

Spun off from [A80_Dutch_Defense.md](https://github.com/onclemarcel/chess_flashcards/blob/main/d4_openings/A80_Dutch_Defense.md), where **2. c4** used to live as a candidate bullet.
