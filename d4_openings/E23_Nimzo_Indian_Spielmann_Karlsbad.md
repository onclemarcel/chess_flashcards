## 7. Bd2 Nxc5 — Stahlberg Variation

[![7... Nxc5](https://backscattering.de/web-boardimage/board.svg?fen=r1bqk2r/pp1p1ppp/2n1p3/2n5/1bP5/1QN2N2/PP1BPPPP/R3KB1R&lastMove=e4c5&coordinates=true&size=280)](https://lichess.org/analysis/standard/r1bqk2r/pp1p1ppp/2n1p3/2n5/1bP5/1QN2N2/PP1BPPPP/R3KB1R_w_KQkq_-_0_8)

Recaptures on c5 with the knight instead, keeping the extra tempo the gambit was offering to buy back. **A real, if unusual, name situation**: `eco.md`'s own entries call this exact position the "San Remo Variation" and a still-deeper line (9. g3) the "Staahlberg Variation" — but Lichess's own live tag calls the *whole* branch, from this leaf all the way through 9. g3, the *Stahlberg Variation* (one 'a') throughout, never using "San Remo" at all. **8. Qc2** is masters' overwhelming reply (100% of a small sample). Not built out further here (backlog) — deeper Stahlberg Variation theory is its own extensive body of work.
