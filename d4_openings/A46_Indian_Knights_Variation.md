# A46 Indian Defense: Knights Variation <br> 1. d4 Nf6 2. Nf3 #

Spun off from [A40's own 1... Nf6 2. Nf3 note](https://github.com/onclemarcel/chess_flashcards/blob/main/d4_openings/A40_QPG.md#_Nf6_Nf3_), which had no candidate bullet pointing anywhere past it. Live-confirmed via the Lichess explorer's own `opening` field: the bare position after 2. Nf3 (before Black's own reply) is already **A46**. White develops the king's knight, keeping the option of c4 (transposing into the A50 Indian complex) in reserve.
