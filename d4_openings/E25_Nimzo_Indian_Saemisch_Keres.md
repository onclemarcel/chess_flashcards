**8... Qa5** is masters' clear main try (73.8%), regaining the pawn with tempo on the c3 pawn and the queenside.

> [!NOTE]
> **8... f5!?**, the *Romanovsky Variation* (+0.3, mention-only), stakes out kingside space instead of regaining the pawn immediately — a real, if secondary, try (24.1% masters). **9. Nh3** is masters' clear main reply (62.7%), developing toward f4/g5 rather than the more natural-looking f3.
