# E20 Nimzo-Indian Defense <br> 1. d4 Nf6 2. c4 e6 3. Nc3 Bb4 #

The Nimzo-Indian is one of the most respected defenses to 1. d4 at every level above club play: Black pins the knight on c3 immediately, threatening to double White's queenside pawns with ... Bxc3 rather than fighting for the centre with a pawn straight away.

### Candidate moves

White's 4th move is a genuine two-way split between the classical positional try and the move that avoids doubled pawns outright:

* **4. e3** (41.1% masters): the Rubinstein System — solid, keeping options open and preparing Bd3/Nf3/O-O before deciding how to meet the pin
* **4. Qc2** (33.6% masters): the Classical Variation — avoids doubled c-pawns altogether if Black ever takes on c3, at the cost of a slower development
* **4. Nf3** (8.0% masters): keeps developing, often transposing back into 4. e3 lines
* **4. f3** (9.1% masters): the Kmoch/Shirov-style try — prepares e4 immediately but weakens the kingside dark squares

None of these are built out yet in this repository (backlog).
