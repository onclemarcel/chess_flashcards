# E20 Nimzo-Indian Defense <br> 1. d4 Nf6 2. c4 e6 3. Nc3 Bb4 #

**Corrected 2026-08-26**: this card used to build White's **4. e3** and **4. Qc2** replies in place, as if they stayed E20. Live-confirmed via the Lichess explorer's own `opening` field: those two moves are actually **E40** (Rubinstein System) and **E32** (Classical Variation) respectively — both well outside the E20-E29 range this batch covers. Moved to [`E40_Nimzo_Indian_Rubinstein.md`](https://github.com/onclemarcel/chess_flashcards/blob/main/d4_openings/E40_Nimzo_Indian_Rubinstein.md) and [`E32_Nimzo_Indian_Classical.md`](https://github.com/onclemarcel/chess_flashcards/blob/main/d4_openings/E32_Nimzo_Indian_Classical.md). This card now covers its own genuinely E20-coded siblings.

The Nimzo-Indian is one of the most respected defenses to 1. d4 at every level above club play: Black pins the knight on c3 immediately, threatening to double White's queenside pawns with ... Bxc3 rather than fighting for the centre with a pawn straight away.
