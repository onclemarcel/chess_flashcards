# A82 Dutch Defense: Staunton Gambit <br> 1. d4 f5 2. e4 #

Spun off from [A80_Dutch_Defense.md](https://github.com/onclemarcel/chess_flashcards/blob/main/d4_openings/A80_Dutch_Defense.md), where **2. e4** used to live as a candidate bullet. Live-confirmed via the Lichess explorer's own `opening` field, cross-checked against [chessopenings.com's ECO reference](https://chessopenings.com/eco/A82): the same class of "wrong root code" split already applied elsewhere in this repo. White offers the e-pawn immediately, betting that the resulting lead in development outweighs the material — engine-verified essentially dead level (0.0), a genuinely sound try rather than a mere trap.
