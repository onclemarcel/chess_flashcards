Resolves the central tension at once, keeping the queen's pressure on c5/the long diagonal rather than leaving the tension for Black to break with ...cxd4. Masters continue here mostly with **8... Nxd5** (68.4%), taking with the knight rather than the pawn or the c-pawn — a real, live-confirmed follow-up, though this position is already at the natural stopping point for this card and isn't built out further here (backlog).
