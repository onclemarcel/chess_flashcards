# A56 Vulture Defense <br> 1. d4 Nf6 2. c4 c5 3. d5 Ne4 #

Spun off from [A40's own 2... c5 note](https://github.com/onclemarcel/chess_flashcards/blob/main/d4_openings/A40_QPG.md#_c5_), which mentioned the [Benko Gambit](https://github.com/onclemarcel/chess_flashcards/blob/main/d4_openings/A57_Benko_Gambit.md) and [Modern Benoni](https://github.com/onclemarcel/chess_flashcards/blob/main/d4_openings/A60_Modern_Benoni.md) forks after 3. d5 but not this third, much rarer try (0.4% masters at that same fork). Rather than accept the space deficit, Black immediately hops the knight into e4 — a tactical try rather than a structural one, since the knight isn't actually defended there and White has to find the right response.
