# D85 Grünfeld Defense: Exchange Variation <br> 1. d4 Nf6 2. c4 g6 3. Nc3 d5 4. cxd5 Nxd5 5. e4 #

The Grünfeld Defense is the Réti-and-hypermodern-flavoured answer to the King's Indian move order: instead of fianchettoing and waiting, Black strikes the centre with ... d5 immediately, inviting White to build a big pawn duo that Black then attacks with pieces (... c5, ... Nc6, ... Bg7 pressure on d4) rather than pawns.
