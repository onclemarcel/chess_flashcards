Spun off from [A40's own 1... ## 2... b6 — Queen's Indian Accelerated

The true Queen's Indian idea — fianchettoing to pressure c4 — played a move earlier than the main [Queen's Indian Defense](https://github.com/onclemarcel/chess_flashcards/blob/main/d4_openings/E12_Queens_Indian.md), before White has committed the king's knight to f3. Distinct from this repo's own [Pseudo Queen's Indian Defense](https://github.com/onclemarcel/chess_flashcards/blob/main/d4_openings/A47_Pseudo_Queens_Indian.md), which is the same idea reached via 2. Nf3 instead of 2. c4.
