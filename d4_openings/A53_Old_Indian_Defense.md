# A53 Old Indian Defense <br> 1. d4 Nf6 2. c4 d6 #

A genuine zero-coverage gap surfaced by a full A00-E99 ECO-code audit: the position sat in [A40's own 1... Nf6 2. c4 stats table](https://github.com/onclemarcel/chess_flashcards/blob/main/d4_openings/A40_QPG.md#_Nf6_c4_) at a real 4.8% masters share with no candidate bullet or card pointing to it — the same "silent gap" pattern as the Trompowsky/Benko/Modern Benoni/Budapest finds before it. Black commits to ... d6 immediately, keeping options for a King's-Indian-style e5 break without first fianchettoing the bishop — a solid, slightly old-fashioned setup (hence the name) that predates the King's Indian's own rise in popularity.
