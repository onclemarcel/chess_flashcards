# chess_flashcards
A quick and dirty set of flash cards on chess theory based on players experience and chess tools.\
\
Use the [authoring guide](./start.md) as a template for creating new content or just look at it to understand how to read the flash cards\
\
The purpose of this documentation is primarily a personnal work to gather information from chess books, web sites, videos, ... with the addition of Lichess statistics, screenshots & Stockfish rating to guide the reader in learning chess theory from players experience.\
\
*DISCLAIMER: I am one of the worst players in chess, but I keep being interested in capturing knowledge on this game. If this work is useful to me, why not sharing it with other people ? This is why this repository is made public, but be certain that the content may not be accurate from the reader's perspective, especially club players, amateurs, or even better ones. That said, contributions are welcome, if any, using the Github tools.*\
\

### From the initial position ###
- **White** opens with:
- [**1. e4** The King's Pawn Game](./e4_openings/B00_KPG.md) to control the center, open lines for its bishop and queen, move one step to the kingside castling. One drawback is that the e4 pawn is not defended after this first move.
- **Black** may play symmetrically with :
- [**... e5**](./e4_openings/C20_KPG.md) to block e4 and get an equal share of the center, *but e5 becomes an undefended pawn*.
- **Black**'s single most popular reply at master level is instead asymmetrical:
- [**... c5**](./e4_openings/B20_Sicilian.md) the Sicilian Defense, fighting for the center on the queenside rather than matching White pawn for pawn. **2. Nf3** (82.7% masters) is the [Open Sicilian (B27)](./e4_openings/B27_Sicilian_Open.md), whose own sidelines — [Mongoose](./e4_openings/B27_Sicilian_Open.md) (2... Qa5), [Quinteros](./e4_openings/B27_Sicilian_Open.md) (2... Qc7), [Katalimov](./e4_openings/B27_Sicilian_Open.md) (2... b6), [Hyperaccelerated Dragon](./e4_openings/B27_Sicilian_Open.md#_g6_) (2... g6), [O'Kelly (B28)](./e4_openings/B28_Sicilian_OKelly.md) (2... a6), and [Nimzowitsch (B29)](./e4_openings/B29_Sicilian_Nimzovich_Rubinstein.md) (2... Nf6) — sit alongside Black's four main systems: the [Najdorf and its own d6-first fork tree](./e4_openings/B50_Sicilian_d6_Open.md) (2... d6), the [Sveshnikov/Taimanov/Rossolimo family](./e4_openings/B30_Sicilian_Nc6_Open.md) (2... Nc6), the [Kan/Taimanov family](./e4_openings/B40_Sicilian_e6_Open.md) (2... e6), and the [Accelerated Dragon](./e4_openings/B34_Sicilian_g6_Accelerated_Dragon.md) (2... Nc6 3. d4 cxd4 4. Nxd4 g6). **2. Nc3** is the [Closed Sicilian (B23)](./e4_openings/B23_Sicilian_Closed.md), forking to the [Grand Prix Attack](./e4_openings/B23_Sicilian_Closed.md#_f4_) (3. f4) and the [Fianchetto Variation (B24)](./e4_openings/B24_Sicilian_Closed_g3.md) (3. g3), running through [B25](./e4_openings/B25_Sicilian_Closed_d6.md) to its own [B26](./e4_openings/B26_Sicilian_Closed_Be3.md). **2. c3** is the [Alapin Variation (B22)](./e4_openings/B22_Sicilian_Alapin.md), and **2. f4**/**2. d4** are the [McDonnell Attack / Smith-Morra Gambit (B21)](./e4_openings/B21_Sicilian_McDonnell_Smith_Morra.md).
  - **2... Nc6**'s own [B30](./e4_openings/B30_Sicilian_Nc6_Open.md) forks to the [Rossolimo Fianchetto (B31)](./e4_openings/B31_Sicilian_Rossolimo_Fianchetto.md) (3. Bb5 g6) and, more critically, the [Open Sicilian (B32)](./e4_openings/B32_Sicilian_Open.md) (3. d4 — even the bare move itself is B32-coded, a "wrong root code" fix), which itself forks to the Flohr/Nimzo-American/Löwenthal-Kalashnikov Variations that stay B32, the [Lasker-Pelikan/Sveshnikov complex (B33)](./e4_openings/B33_Sicilian_Lasker_Pelikan.md) (4... Nf6), and the true [Accelerated Dragon (B34)](./e4_openings/B34_Sicilian_g6_Accelerated_Dragon.md) (4... g6) — forking further into the [Modern Bc4 Variation (B35)](./e4_openings/B35_Sicilian_Accelerated_Dragon_Bc4.md) and the [Maróczy Bind complex (B36](./e4_openings/B36_Sicilian_Accelerated_Dragon_Maroczy.md)-[B39)](./e4_openings/B39_Sicilian_Accelerated_Dragon_Maroczy_Breyer.md), where masters actually prefer 5. c4 (56.2%) over either of B34's own two named lines.
  - **2... e6**'s own [B40](./e4_openings/B40_Sicilian_e6_Open.md) gained the *Marshall Counterattack* (3... d5) and a full *Pin Variation* tree (4... Nf6 5. Nc3 Bb4, forking to the *Koch* and *Jaffe* Variations — masters actually prefer Koch, 82.3%, over the eco.md-first-listed Jaffe) alongside its own existing Open Sicilian fork, and its two deep branches were split out properly: the [Kan Variation (B41](./e4_openings/B41_Sicilian_Kan_Variation.md)-[B43)](./e4_openings/B43_Sicilian_Kan_Knight_Variation.md) (4... a6, forking to the [Modern Variation (B42)](./e4_openings/B42_Sicilian_Kan_Modern_Variation.md) and [Knight Variation (B43)](./e4_openings/B43_Sicilian_Kan_Knight_Variation.md), plus its own Maróczy Bind staying B41) and the [Taimanov Variation (B44](./e4_openings/B44_Sicilian_Taimanov_Szen.md)-[B49)](./e4_openings/B49_Sicilian_Taimanov_a6_Be2.md) (4... Nc6, forking to the *Szén Variation* that stays B44 and the [5. Nc3 tabiya (B45)](./e4_openings/B45_Sicilian_Taimanov_Nc3.md) — itself forking to [B46](./e4_openings/B46_Sicilian_Taimanov_a6.md), the *Bastrikov Variation* ([B47](./e4_openings/B47_Sicilian_Taimanov_Bastrikov.md)-[B49](./e4_openings/B49_Sicilian_Taimanov_a6_Be2.md)), and the *American Attack* (a sharp, forcing 6. Ndb5 Bb4 7. Nd6+ that doesn't actually win material, verified move-by-move rather than assumed)).
  - **2... d6**'s own [B50](./e4_openings/B50_Sicilian_d6_Open.md) is much shallower than it once looked (another "wrong root code" fix — its own former "5. Nc3" root was already **B54/B56**, several codes past B50 itself): the real B50 is just the root plus the *Wing Gambit, Deferred* (3. b4), forking to the [Moscow Variation (B51](./e4_openings/B51_Sicilian_Moscow_Variation.md)-[B52)](./e4_openings/B52_Sicilian_Moscow_Bd7.md) (3. Bb5+) and the [Modern Variations Main Line (B54)](./e4_openings/B54_Sicilian_d6_Modern_Main_Line.md) (3. d4), the latter itself forking to the [Prins Variation (B55)](./e4_openings/B55_Sicilian_Prins_Venice_Attack.md) (5. f3) and the [Classical Variation (B56)](./e4_openings/B56_Sicilian_Classical_Variation.md) (5. Nc3, the true home of the Najdorf/Dragon/Scheveningen sub-trees — each flagged as its own out-of-range B90/B70/B80 code for a future batch, not yet split into its own card) — with B56's own 5... Nc6 forking further into the [Sozin Attack (B57)](./e4_openings/B57_Sicilian_Sozin_Attack.md) (6. Bc4, home of the *Magnus Smith Trap*). The [Chekhover Variation (B53)](./e4_openings/B53_Sicilian_Chekhover.md) (3. d4 cxd4 4. Qxd4) is its own independent sideline off the same 3. d4 fork. The Classical System reached via the *Nc6-first* move order (2... Nc6 before ... d6) gets its own separate codes, [B58](./e4_openings/B58_Sicilian_Classical_System.md)-[B59](./e4_openings/B59_Sicilian_Boleslavsky_Nb3.md), even where it transposes into B56's own tabiya.
- **Black** may also prepare ... d5 before playing it:
- [**... e6**](./e4_openings/C00_French_Defense.md) the French Defense, built out to the [Winawer/Classical](./e4_openings/C00_French_Defense.md#_Nc3_) fork after 2. d4 d5 3. Nc3, or
- [**... c6**](./e4_openings/B10_Caro_Kann.md) the Caro-Kann Defense, which keeps the light-squared bishop free — 2. Nc3 d5 3. Nf3 Bg4 is [B11](./e4_openings/B11_Caro_Kann_Two_Knights_Mindeno.md), the *Mindeno Variation*. Masters' main try, 2. d4, is [B12](./e4_openings/B12_Caro_Kann.md), forking to the Advance Variation (3. e5), the [Exchange Variation](./e4_openings/B13_Caro_Kann_Exchange_Panov.md) (3. exd5), whose own Panov-Botvinnik Attack forks further to [B14](./e4_openings/B14_Caro_Kann_Panov_Attack.md), and the [Classical/Modern Variation](./e4_openings/B15_Caro_Kann_Classical_Modern.md) (3. Nc3), which forks to the [Bronstein-Larsen](./e4_openings/B16_Caro_Kann_Bronstein_Larsen.md) (B16), [Karpov](./e4_openings/B17_Caro_Kann_Karpov_Variation.md) (B17), and [Classical](./e4_openings/B18_Caro_Kann_Classical_Variation.md) (B18, itself forking to the [Spassky Variation](./e4_openings/B19_Caro_Kann_Spassky_Variation.md), B19) Variations.
- **Black** may instead delay the centre entirely (hypermodern tries — rare, but sound and genuinely offbeat):
- [**... d6**](./e4_openings/B07_Pirc_Defense.md) the Pirc Defense, forking into the [Austrian Attack](./e4_openings/B09_Pirc_Austrian_Attack.md) (4. f4) / [Classical](./e4_openings/B08_Pirc_Classical_System.md) (4. Nf3) / [150 Attack](./e4_openings/B07_Pirc_Defense.md#_Be3_) (4. Be3) after 2. d4 Nf6 3. Nc3 g6,
- [**... g6**](./e4_openings/B06_Modern_Defense.md) the Modern (Robatsch) Defense, the same idea reached by fianchettoing first, or
- [**... Nf6**](./e4_openings/B02_Alekhine_Defense.md) the Alekhine Defense, provoking White's centre forward instead of contesting it — leads to the [Four Pawns Attack](./e4_openings/B03_Alekhine_Defense.md#_f4_) if White pushes all the way, or to the [Modern Variation, Main Line](./e4_openings/B05_Alekhine_Modern_Bg4.md) (its own code, B05) after 3. d4 d6 4. Nf3 Bg4.

### From 1. e4 e5 ###
- **White** may develop its pieces with :
  - [**2. Nc3** The Vienna game](./e4_openings/C25_Vienna_Game.md)
  - [**2. Bc4** The Bishop's Opening](./e4_openings/C23_Bishop_Opening.md)
- **White** can play for tricks with :
  - [**2. Qh5** The Wayward Queen](./e4_openings/C20_Wayward_Queen.md)
- **White** may attack the undefended e5 pawn with :
  - [**2. f4** The King's Gambit](./e4_openings/C30_King_Gambit.md)
  - [**2. d4** The Center Game](./e4_openings/C20_Center_Game.md)
  - [**2. Nf3** The King's Knight Opening](./e4_openings/C40_King_Knight.md).

### From 1. e4 e5 2. Nf3 ###
- **Black** can the counter-attack on e4 with :
  - [**2. Nf3 f5** The Latvian Gambit](./gambits/Latvian/Latvian.md),
  - [**2. Nf3 d5** The Elephant Gambit](./gambits/Elephant/Elephant.md)
  - [**2. Nf3 Nf6** The Petrov's Defense](./e4_openings/C42_Petrov_Defense.md)
- **Black** can otherwise defend the e5-pawn with
  - [**2. Nf3 d6** The Philidor Defense](./e4_openings/C41_Philidor_Defense.md) 
  - [**2. Nf3 Nc6** The King's Knight Opening](./e4_openings/C44_Nc6_King_Knight.md)

### From 1. e4 e5 2. Nf3 Nc6 ###
- **White** is able to enter into well-known openings such as:
  - [**3. Bb5**, the Spanish Opening or Ruy Lopez](./e4_openings/C60_Ruy_Lopez.md), masters' clear favourite,
  - [**3. Bc4**, the Italian Opening](./e4_openings/C50_Italian.md),
  - [**3. d4**, the Scotch Opening](./e4_openings/C44_Scotch.md),
  - [**3. Nc3**, the Four Knights Game](./e4_openings/C47_Four_Knights_Game.md).
  - [**3. c3**, the Ponziani Opening](./e4_openings/C44_Ponziani.md), a genuine rarity today (0.6% masters) preparing an immediate d4.

### From the initial position, White's other first moves ###
- [**1. d4** The Queen's Pawn Game](./d4_openings/A40_QPG.md), White's second most popular try (35.8% masters):
  - **1... Nf6** (60.9% masters) leads into the Indian Defense complex: [King's Indian](./d4_openings/E70_Kings_Indian.md) / [Grünfeld](./d4_openings/D85_Grunfeld.md) fork after 2. c4 g6 3. Nc3 (or, if White delays Nc3 for an early fianchetto instead, the [Neo-Grünfeld Defense](./d4_openings/D71_Neo_Grunfeld_Defense.md) after 3. g3 d5), or [Nimzo-Indian](./d4_openings/E20_Nimzo_Indian.md) (forking to the [Three Knights](./d4_openings/E21_Nimzo_Indian_Three_Knights.md) (E21), [Spielmann](./d4_openings/E22_Nimzo_Indian_Spielmann.md) (E22, running to its own [Romanovsky Gambit, E23](./d4_openings/E23_Nimzo_Indian_Spielmann_Karlsbad.md)), and [Sämisch](./d4_openings/E24_Nimzo_Indian_Saemisch.md) (E24, the deepest chain in this range, running through [E25](./d4_openings/E25_Nimzo_Indian_Saemisch_Keres.md)/[E26](./d4_openings/E26_Nimzo_Indian_Saemisch_OKelly.md)/[E27](./d4_openings/E27_Nimzo_Indian_Saemisch_OO.md)/[E28](./d4_openings/E28_Nimzo_Indian_Saemisch_e3.md) to its own [Main Line, E29](./d4_openings/E29_Nimzo_Indian_Saemisch_Main_Line.md)) Variations, while masters' own two main tries — [4. e3, the Rubinstein System (E40)](./d4_openings/E40_Nimzo_Indian_Rubinstein.md) (forking to [4... c5 (E41)](./d4_openings/E41_Nimzo_Indian_Rubinstein_c5.md), itself forking to [E42](./d4_openings/E42_Nimzo_Indian_Rubinstein_Ne2.md); the live-renamed [St. Petersburg Variation (E43)](./d4_openings/E43_Nimzo_Indian_St_Petersburg.md), running through [E44](./d4_openings/E44_Nimzo_Indian_St_Petersburg_Ne2.md) to its own [Fischer Variation, E45](./d4_openings/E45_Nimzo_Indian_St_Petersburg_Fischer.md) — a genuine name swap with `eco.md`, whose own "Fischer Variation" label belongs at E43's root instead; and the [Normal Variation (E46)](./d4_openings/E46_Nimzo_Indian_Rubinstein_Normal.md), running through [E47](./d4_openings/E47_Nimzo_Indian_Rubinstein_Bd3.md)/[E48](./d4_openings/E48_Nimzo_Indian_Rubinstein_Classical.md) to its own [Botvinnik System, E49](./d4_openings/E49_Nimzo_Indian_Rubinstein_Botvinnik.md)) and [4. Qc2, the Classical Variation (E32)](./d4_openings/E32_Nimzo_Indian_Classical.md) (itself forking to the [Zurich](./d4_openings/E33_Nimzo_Indian_Classical_Zurich.md) (E33), [Noa](./d4_openings/E34_Nimzo_Indian_Classical_Noa.md) (E34, running through [E35](./d4_openings/E35_Nimzo_Indian_Classical_Noa_Exchange.md)/[E36](./d4_openings/E36_Nimzo_Indian_Classical_Noa_Main_Line.md) to its own [Main Line, E37](./d4_openings/E37_Nimzo_Indian_Classical_Noa_Qc2.md)), and [Berlin](./d4_openings/E38_Nimzo_Indian_Classical_Berlin.md) (E38, running to its own [Pirc Variation, E39](./d4_openings/E39_Nimzo_Indian_Classical_Berlin_Pirc.md)) Variations) — sit in later ECO ranges entirely, alongside [4. Bg5, the Leningrad Variation (E30)](./d4_openings/E30_Nimzo_Indian_Leningrad.md) (running to its own [Benoni Defense, E31](./d4_openings/E31_Nimzo_Indian_Leningrad_Benoni.md)) / [Anti-Nimzo-Indian (E10)](./d4_openings/E10_Anti_Nimzo_Indian.md) (3. Be7, E06-E09, ending at the [Main Line (E09)](./d4_openings/E09_Catalan_Closed_Main_Line.md)). After 2. c4 d6, the [Old Indian Defense (A53)](./d4_openings/A53_Old_Indian_Defense.md) forks into the [Ukrainian Variation (A54)](./d4_openings/A54_Old_Indian_Ukrainian_Variation.md) (3. Nf3 (the **A46** Knights Variation — its own 2... e6 forks into the Torre Attack/Yusupov-Rubinstein System) instead, Black's 2... b6 reaches the [Pseudo Queen's Indian Defense (A47)](./d4_openings/A47_Pseudo_Queens_Indian.md) (one ply further, 4... c5 is the *Marienbad System*), 2... g6 the [East Indian Defense (A48)](./d4_openings/A48_East_Indian_Defense.md) (forking into its own Torre Attack/London System, or the [Przepiorka Variation (A49)](./d4_openings/A49_Indian_Przepiorka_Variation.md) after 3. g3), or 2... Ne4 the [Döry Defense](./d4_openings/A46_Indian_Knights_Variation.md). Rarer still: 1... Nc6 the [Mikenas Defense](./d4_openings/A40_Mikenas_Defense.md), 1... b5 the [Polish Defense](./d4_openings/A40_Polish_Defense.md), 1... b6 the [English Defense](./d4_openings/A40_QPG.md#_b6_), 2... b6 (after 2. c4) the [Queen's Indian Accelerated](./d4_openings/A50_Indian_Normal_Variation.md), and 2... Nc6 (after 2. c4) the [Mexican Defense](./d4_openings/A50_Indian_Normal_Variation.md#_Nc6_).
  - **1... d6** (2.8% masters) is the [Queen's Pawn Game (A41)](./d4_openings/A41_Queens_Pawn_Game.md) — delays every commitment, transposing toward the Old Indian Defense (2. c4, forking into the [Modern Defense: Averbakh System (A42)](./d4_openings/A42_Modern_Averbakh_System.md) after 2... g6 3. Nc3 Bg7 4. e4), the Pirc Defense (2. e4), or staying flexible with 2. Nf3.
  - **1... d5** (25.8% masters) opens the [Queen's Gambit](./d4_openings/D06_Queens_Gambit.md) after 2. c4 (Slav 49.5% / QGD 35.3% / QGA 11.8%) — the QGD itself forking into the Orthodox/Classical complex (3. Nc3 Nf6) or the [Tarrasch Defense](./d4_openings/D32_Tarrasch_Defense.md) (3. Nc3 c5) — or, if White delays c4, the [Queen's Pawn Game hub (D00)](./d4_openings/D00_Queens_Pawn_Game.md) — 2. Nc3 (the Chigorin Variation, forking into the [Richter-Veresov Attack](./d4_openings/D01_Richter_Veresov_Attack.md) after 2... Nf6 3. Bg5, or the Rapport-Jobava System after 3. Bf4), 2. Bf4 (the Accelerated London System), and 2. Bg5 (the Levitsky Attack) — or the [Zukertort Variation](./d4_openings/D02_Zukertort_Variation.md) (2. Nf3), which itself forks into the [Torre Attack](./d4_openings/D03_Torre_Attack.md), the [Colle System](./d4_openings/D04_Colle_System.md) (3. e3, forking into D04's own c5/Bf5/g6/Bg4 replies and [D05](./d4_openings/D05_Colle_System.md)'s 3... e6 tabiya), and the [London System](./d4_openings/D02_London_System.md) (3. Bf4) — genuine sound-but-rarely-faced sidesteps out of mainstream Queen's Gambit/Slav theory, the London arguably the most commonly *faced* of the four despite its modest master-level share.
  - **1... f5** opens the [Dutch Defense (A80)](./d4_openings/A80_Dutch_Defense.md) — masters' clear favourite reply, [2. g3](./d4_openings/A81_Dutch_Fianchetto_Attack.md) (40.9%, the Fianchetto Attack), forks into the Semi-Leningrad Variation (2... Nf6 3. g3 g6, forking to the Warsaw and Matulovic Variations after 7. Nc3) and the [Classical/Stonewall complex (A90-A99)](./d4_openings/A90_Dutch_Classical_and_Stonewall.md) (3... e6 4. Bg2, forking to the Nimzo-Dutch Variation, the Stonewall Variation with its Botvinnik/Chekhover sub-lines, and the Classical Variation's own Ilyin-Zhenevsky attacking plan). White can also offer the [Staunton Gambit (A82-A83)](./d4_openings/A82_Dutch_Staunton_Gambit.md) (2. e4, engine-verified dead level, forking further after 4. Bg5 into the Alekhine/Lasker/Chigorin/Nimzowitsch Variations) or the [Hopton Attack](./d4_openings/A80_Dutch_Defense.md#_Bg5_) (2. Bg5).
  - **1... c5** opens the [Old Benoni (A43)](./d4_openings/A43_Old_Benoni.md) — a real surprise here: masters' own most popular reply at move 2 (2... e5, the move the line is named for) is objectively the *worst* of Black's realistic options per Stockfish, covered separately on the [A44](./d4_openings/A44_Old_Benoni.md) card the code actually belongs to.
