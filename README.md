# chess_flashcards
A quick and dirty set of flash cards on chess theory based on players experience and chess tools.\
\
Use the [authoring guide](./start.md) as a template for creating new content or just look at it to understand how to read the flash cards\
\
The purpose of this documentation is primarily a personnal work to gather information from chess books, web sites, videos, ... with the addition of Lichess statistics, screenshots & Stockfish rating to guide the reader in learning chess theory from players experience.\
\
*DISCLAIMER: I am one of the worst players in chess, but I keep being interested in capturing knowledge on this game. If this work is useful to me, why not sharing it with other people ? This is why this repository is made public, but be certain that the content may not be accurate from the reader's perspective, especially club players, amateurs, or even better ones. That said, contributions are welcome, if any, using the Github tools.*\
\

### From the initial position ###
- **White** opens with:
- [**1. e4** The King's Pawn Game](./e4_openings/B00_KPG.md) to control the center, open lines for its bishop and queen, move one step to the kingside castling. One drawback is that the e4 pawn is not defended after this first move.
- **Black** may play symmetrically with :
- [**... e5**](./e4_openings/C20_KPG.md) to block e4 and get an equal share of the center, *but e5 becomes an undefended pawn*.
- **Black**'s single most popular reply at master level is instead asymmetrical:
- [**... c5**](./e4_openings/B20_Sicilian.md) the Sicilian Defense, fighting for the center on the queenside rather than matching White pawn for pawn. **2. Nf3** (82.7% masters) is the [Open Sicilian (B27)](./e4_openings/B27_Sicilian_Open.md), whose own sidelines — [Mongoose](./e4_openings/B27_Sicilian_Open.md) (2... Qa5), [Quinteros](./e4_openings/B27_Sicilian_Open.md) (2... Qc7), [Katalimov](./e4_openings/B27_Sicilian_Open.md) (2... b6), [Hyperaccelerated Dragon](./e4_openings/B27_Sicilian_Open.md#_g6_) (2... g6), [O'Kelly (B28)](./e4_openings/B28_Sicilian_OKelly.md) (2... a6), and [Nimzowitsch (B29)](./e4_openings/B29_Sicilian_Nimzovich_Rubinstein.md) (2... Nf6) — sit alongside Black's four main systems: the [Najdorf and its own d6-first fork tree](./e4_openings/B50_Sicilian_d6_Open.md) (2... d6), the [Sveshnikov/Taimanov/Rossolimo family](./e4_openings/B30_Sicilian_Nc6_Open.md) (2... Nc6), the [Kan/Taimanov family](./e4_openings/B40_Sicilian_e6_Open.md) (2... e6), and the [Accelerated Dragon](./e4_openings/B34_Sicilian_g6_Accelerated_Dragon.md) (2... Nc6 3. d4 cxd4 4. Nxd4 g6). **2. Nc3** is the [Closed Sicilian (B23)](./e4_openings/B23_Sicilian_Closed.md), forking to the [Grand Prix Attack](./e4_openings/B23_Sicilian_Closed.md#_f4_) (3. f4) and the [Fianchetto Variation (B24)](./e4_openings/B24_Sicilian_Closed_g3.md) (3. g3), running through [B25](./e4_openings/B25_Sicilian_Closed_d6.md) to its own [B26](./e4_openings/B26_Sicilian_Closed_Be3.md). **2. c3** is the [Alapin Variation (B22)](./e4_openings/B22_Sicilian_Alapin.md), and **2. f4**/**2. d4** are the [McDonnell Attack / Smith-Morra Gambit (B21)](./e4_openings/B21_Sicilian_McDonnell_Smith_Morra.md).
  - **2... Nc6**'s own [B30](./e4_openings/B30_Sicilian_Nc6_Open.md) forks to the [Rossolimo Fianchetto (B31)](./e4_openings/B31_Sicilian_Rossolimo_Fianchetto.md) (3. Bb5 g6) and, more critically, the [Open Sicilian (B32)](./e4_openings/B32_Sicilian_Open.md) (3. d4 — even the bare move itself is B32-coded, a "wrong root code" fix), which itself forks to the Flohr/Nimzo-American/Löwenthal-Kalashnikov Variations that stay B32, the [Lasker-Pelikan/Sveshnikov complex (B33)](./e4_openings/B33_Sicilian_Lasker_Pelikan.md) (4... Nf6), and the true [Accelerated Dragon (B34)](./e4_openings/B34_Sicilian_g6_Accelerated_Dragon.md) (4... g6) — forking further into the [Modern Bc4 Variation (B35)](./e4_openings/B35_Sicilian_Accelerated_Dragon_Bc4.md) and the [Maróczy Bind complex (B36](./e4_openings/B36_Sicilian_Accelerated_Dragon_Maroczy.md)-[B39)](./e4_openings/B39_Sicilian_Accelerated_Dragon_Maroczy_Breyer.md), where masters actually prefer 5. c4 (56.2%) over either of B34's own two named lines.
  - **2... e6**'s own [B40](./e4_openings/B40_Sicilian_e6_Open.md) gained the *Marshall Counterattack* (3... d5) and a full *Pin Variation* tree (4... Nf6 5. Nc3 Bb4, forking to the *Koch* and *Jaffe* Variations — masters actually prefer Koch, 82.3%, over the eco.md-first-listed Jaffe) alongside its own existing Open Sicilian fork, and its two deep branches were split out properly: the [Kan Variation (B41](./e4_openings/B41_Sicilian_Kan_Variation.md)-[B43)](./e4_openings/B43_Sicilian_Kan_Knight_Variation.md) (4... a6, forking to the [Modern Variation (B42)](./e4_openings/B42_Sicilian_Kan_Modern_Variation.md) and [Knight Variation (B43)](./e4_openings/B43_Sicilian_Kan_Knight_Variation.md), plus its own Maróczy Bind staying B41) and the [Taimanov Variation (B44](./e4_openings/B44_Sicilian_Taimanov_Szen.md)-[B49)](./e4_openings/B49_Sicilian_Taimanov_a6_Be2.md) (4... Nc6, forking to the *Szén Variation* that stays B44 and the [5. Nc3 tabiya (B45)](./e4_openings/B45_Sicilian_Taimanov_Nc3.md) — itself forking to [B46](./e4_openings/B46_Sicilian_Taimanov_a6.md), the *Bastrikov Variation* ([B47](./e4_openings/B47_Sicilian_Taimanov_Bastrikov.md)-[B49](./e4_openings/B49_Sicilian_Taimanov_a6_Be2.md)), and the *American Attack* (a sharp, forcing 6. Ndb5 Bb4 7. Nd6+ that doesn't actually win material, verified move-by-move rather than assumed)).
  - **2... d6**'s own [B50](./e4_openings/B50_Sicilian_d6_Open.md) is much shallower than it once looked (another "wrong root code" fix — its own former "5. Nc3" root was already **B54/B56**, several codes past B50 itself): the real B50 is just the root plus the *Wing Gambit, Deferred* (3. b4), forking to the [Moscow Variation (B51](./e4_openings/B51_Sicilian_Moscow_Variation.md)-[B52)](./e4_openings/B52_Sicilian_Moscow_Bd7.md) (3. Bb5+) and the [Modern Variations Main Line (B54)](./e4_openings/B54_Sicilian_d6_Modern_Main_Line.md) (3. d4), the latter itself forking to the [Prins Variation (B55)](./e4_openings/B55_Sicilian_Prins_Venice_Attack.md) (5. f3) and the [Classical Variation (B56)](./e4_openings/B56_Sicilian_Classical_Variation.md) (5. Nc3, the true home of the Najdorf/Scheveningen sub-trees — each properly re-rooted at its own B90/B80 code, below) — with B56's own 5... Nc6 forking further into the [Sozin Attack (B57)](./e4_openings/B57_Sicilian_Sozin_Attack.md) (6. Bc4, home of the *Magnus Smith Trap*) and the whole [Richter-Rauzer complex (B60](./e4_openings/B60_Sicilian_Richter_Rauzer.md)-[B69)](./e4_openings/B69_Sicilian_Richter_Rauzer_Bxf6.md) (6. Bg5, masters' actual main try there) — one of the most heavily analysed systems in the whole Sicilian: root plus its own *Dragon*/*Modern* Variations ([B61](./e4_openings/B61_Sicilian_Richter_Rauzer_Larsen.md)), forking to the [Rauzer Attack tabiya (B62](./e4_openings/B62_Sicilian_Richter_Rauzer_e6.md)-[B63)](./e4_openings/B63_Sicilian_Richter_Rauzer_Traditional.md) (7. Qd2, 92% masters — well ahead of the *Keres*/*Margate*/*Podebrady*/*Richter Attack* sidelines that stay B62), itself forking to the *Classical Variation* ([B64](./e4_openings/B64_Sicilian_Richter_Rauzer_f4.md)-[B65)](./e4_openings/B65_Sicilian_Richter_Rauzer_Nxd4.md) (7... Be7) and the *Neo-Modern Variation* ([B66](./e4_openings/B66_Sicilian_Richter_Rauzer_Neo_Modern.md)-[B69)](./e4_openings/B69_Sicilian_Richter_Rauzer_Bxf6.md) (7... a6, masters' real preference at that fork). B56's own 5... g6 also forks into the whole [Dragon Variation (B70](./e4_openings/B70_Sicilian_Dragon.md)-[B79)](./e4_openings/B79_Sicilian_Dragon_Yugoslav_h4.md), now re-rooted and fully built out: root + [Levenfish (B71)](./e4_openings/B71_Sicilian_Dragon_Levenfish.md) (6. f4, a real minority at only 1.7% masters) fork to the [Classical Variation (B72](./e4_openings/B72_Sicilian_Dragon_Be3.md)-[B74)](./e4_openings/B74_Sicilian_Dragon_Classical_Nb3.md) (7. Be2, with the *Amsterdam*/*Grigoriev*/*Nottingham*/*Zollner Gambit*/*Stockholm Attack*/*Spielmann*/*Bernard Defense* sub-lines all named) and the [Yugoslav Attack (B75](./e4_openings/B75_Sicilian_Dragon_Yugoslav.md)-[B79)](./e4_openings/B79_Sicilian_Dragon_Yugoslav_h4.md) (7. f3, one of the sharpest systems in all of chess, running through the *Modern Line*/*Main Line* tabiyas down to the deeply analysed 12. h4 position). B56's own 5... e6 forks into the whole [Scheveningen/Sozin complex (B80](./e4_openings/B80_Sicilian_Scheveningen.md)-[B89)](./e4_openings/B89_Sicilian_Sozin_Be3.md): the root plus the *Vitolins* (6. Bb5) and *Fianchetto* (6. g3) sidelines, forking to the [Keres Attack (B81)](./e4_openings/B81_Sicilian_Scheveningen_Keres.md) (6. g4), [6. f4 and the Tal Variation (B82)](./e4_openings/B82_Sicilian_Scheveningen_f4.md), and masters' own main try [6. Be2 (B83](./e4_openings/B83_Sicilian_Scheveningen_Be2.md)-[B85)](./e4_openings/B85_Sicilian_Scheveningen_Classical_f4.md) — itself forking to the Modern Scheveningen (6... Nc6) and, more critically, the [Classical Variation (B84-B85)](./e4_openings/B84_Sicilian_Scheveningen_Classical_a6.md) (6... a6, including the *Maroczy System*) — while 6. Bc4 leaves the Scheveningen structure entirely for the [Sozin Attack (B86](./e4_openings/B86_Sicilian_Scheveningen_Sozin.md)-[B89)](./e4_openings/B89_Sicilian_Sozin_Be3.md), forking to the *Flank Variation* (B87, 6... a6 7. Bb3 b5) and the [Leonhardt/Fischer Variations (B88)](./e4_openings/B88_Sicilian_Sozin_Leonhardt.md) (6... Nc6), running to its own [7. Be3 and the Velimirovic Attack (B89)](./e4_openings/B89_Sicilian_Sozin_Be3.md). B56's own 5... a6, the *Najdorf Variation*, is by far masters' single most respected system in the whole Sicilian — the [Najdorf complex (B90](./e4_openings/B90_Sicilian_Najdorf.md)-[B99)](./e4_openings/B99_Sicilian_Najdorf_Main_Line.md): root plus the *Adams Attack* (6. h3) and *Lipnitzky Attack* (6. Bc4) sidelines and the [English Attack (B90)](./e4_openings/B90_Sicilian_Najdorf.md) (6. Be3), forking to the [Zagreb/Fianchetto Variation (B91)](./e4_openings/B91_Sicilian_Najdorf_Zagreb.md) (6. g3), the [Opocensky Variation (B92)](./e4_openings/B92_Sicilian_Najdorf_Opocensky.md) (6. Be2), the [Amsterdam Variation (B93)](./e4_openings/B93_Sicilian_Najdorf_Amsterdam.md) (6. f4), and masters' own main try [6. Bg5 (B94](./e4_openings/B94_Sicilian_Najdorf_Bg5.md)-[B99)](./e4_openings/B99_Sicilian_Najdorf_Main_Line.md) — itself forking to the *Ivkov Variation* (B94) and, after 6... e6 [(B95)](./e4_openings/B95_Sicilian_Najdorf_e6.md), masters' overwhelming try [7. f4 (B96](./e4_openings/B96_Sicilian_Najdorf_f4.md)-[B99)](./e4_openings/B99_Sicilian_Najdorf_Main_Line.md) — the root of the whole Poisoned-Pawn/Browne/Goteborg complex, forking to the *Polugaevsky Variation* (B96, running to its own *Simagin Line*), the [Poisoned Pawn Variation (B97)](./e4_openings/B97_Sicilian_Najdorf_Poisoned_Pawn.md) (7... Qb6, one of the most concretely analysed, memorization-heavy lines in all of chess theory), and [7... Be7 (B98](./e4_openings/B98_Sicilian_Najdorf_Be7.md)-[B99)](./e4_openings/B99_Sicilian_Najdorf_Main_Line.md) — forking to the *Browne*/*Goteborg* Variations (B98) and, via the *Traditional Line* (8... Qc7), the deepest, most heavily analysed tabiya in the whole complex, the [Main Line (B99)](./e4_openings/B99_Sicilian_Najdorf_Main_Line.md) — closing out the entire B00-B99 Sicilian Defense range. The [Chekhover Variation (B53)](./e4_openings/B53_Sicilian_Chekhover.md) (3. d4 cxd4 4. Qxd4) is its own independent sideline off the same 3. d4 fork. The Classical System reached via the *Nc6-first* move order (2... Nc6 before ... d6) gets its own separate codes, [B58](./e4_openings/B58_Sicilian_Classical_System.md)-[B59](./e4_openings/B59_Sicilian_Boleslavsky_Nb3.md), even where it transposes into B56's own tabiya.
- **Black** may also prepare ... d5 before playing it:
- [**... e6**](./e4_openings/C00_French_Defense.md) the French Defense, now fully swept C00-C19: after 2. d4 d5, White's own three sidesteps of the Nc3 main line are each their own code — the [Exchange Variation (C01)](./e4_openings/C01_French_Exchange.md) (3. exd5, simple and drawish, with the *Svenonius*/*Bogoljubow* sidelines), the [Advance Variation (C02)](./e4_openings/C02_French_Advance.md) (3. e5, locking the centre — forking to the *Paulsen Attack* (5. Nf3) itself split between the *Euwe Variation* (5... Bd7) and the *Milner-Barry Gambit* (5... Qb6 6. Bd3, genuinely dead level despite the sacrificed pawn), and the *Wade Variation*), and the huge [Tarrasch Variation complex (C03](./e4_openings/C03_French_Tarrasch.md)-[C09)](./e4_openings/C09_French_Tarrasch_Open_Main_Line.md) (3. Nd2, avoiding the coming pin) — itself forking to the [Guimard Variation (C03-C04)](./e4_openings/C04_French_Tarrasch_Guimard.md) (3... Nc6), the [Closed Variation (C05-C06)](./e4_openings/C05_French_Tarrasch_Closed.md) (3... Nf6, running through the *Botvinnik*/*Leningrad* Variations to its own deeply analysed *Main Line*), and the [Open Variation (C07-C09)](./e4_openings/C07_French_Tarrasch_Open.md) (3... c5, with the *Eliskases Variation* off 4... Qxd5, running to its own *Main Line*). Masters' own main try, **3. Nc3**, forks between the [Classical Variation (C11-C14)](./e4_openings/C11_French_Classical.md) (3... Nf6, live-tagged that name a full ply earlier than `eco.md`'s own bare label) — itself forking to the *Steinitz Variation* (4. e5, masters' actual slight preference, with the *Bradford*/*Boleslavsky*/*Gledhill Attack*/*Brodsky-Jones* sub-lines all named) and 4. Bg5, splitting further into the [MacCutcheon Variation (C12)](./e4_openings/C12_French_MacCutcheon.md) (4... Bb4, one of the most theoretically dense tries in the whole French, with the *Lasker*/*Duras*/*Janowski*/*Bernstein*/*Dr. Olland*/*Chigorin*/*Grigoriev* Variations) and the [Classical proper (C13-C14)](./e4_openings/C13_French_Classical.md) (4... Be7, running through the *Chatard-Alekhine Attack*'s own *Maroczy*/*Breyer*/*Teichmann*/*Spielmann* Variations to its own deepest *Steinitz*/*Stahlberg* tabiya) — and the [Winawer Variation (C15-C19)](./e4_openings/C15_French_Winawer.md) (3... Bb4, masters' single most popular try at the whole 3... fork, and the French's own most theoretically critical line), forking to the *Alekhine-Maróczy Gambit* (4. Ne2, with the *Alatortsev*/*Kan* Variations) and masters' own overwhelming main try, the huge [Advance Variation (C16-C19)](./e4_openings/C16_French_Winawer_Advance.md) (4. e5) — running through the *Petrosian*/*Bogolyubov*/*Russian*/*Rauzer* sidelines to the [Classical Variation (C18)](./e4_openings/C18_French_Winawer_Advance_Classical.md) (6. bxc3 Qc7) and on to the deepest, most heavily analysed tabiya in the whole French, the [Main Line (C19)](./e4_openings/C19_French_Winawer_Advance_Main_Line.md) (6... Ne7), forking to the *Smyslov*/*positional Main Line* tries and masters' own main try, the *Poisoned Pawn Variation* (7. Qg4) — whose own *Euwe-Gligoric*/*Konstantinopolsky* Variations, genuinely dead level per Stockfish despite White grabbing two pawns, close out the whole range, or
- [**... c6**](./e4_openings/B10_Caro_Kann.md) the Caro-Kann Defense, which keeps the light-squared bishop free — 2. Nc3 d5 3. Nf3 Bg4 is [B11](./e4_openings/B11_Caro_Kann_Two_Knights_Mindeno.md), the *Mindeno Variation*. Masters' main try, 2. d4, is [B12](./e4_openings/B12_Caro_Kann.md), forking to the Advance Variation (3. e5), the [Exchange Variation](./e4_openings/B13_Caro_Kann_Exchange_Panov.md) (3. exd5), whose own Panov-Botvinnik Attack forks further to [B14](./e4_openings/B14_Caro_Kann_Panov_Attack.md), and the [Classical/Modern Variation](./e4_openings/B15_Caro_Kann_Classical_Modern.md) (3. Nc3), which forks to the [Bronstein-Larsen](./e4_openings/B16_Caro_Kann_Bronstein_Larsen.md) (B16), [Karpov](./e4_openings/B17_Caro_Kann_Karpov_Variation.md) (B17), and [Classical](./e4_openings/B18_Caro_Kann_Classical_Variation.md) (B18, itself forking to the [Spassky Variation](./e4_openings/B19_Caro_Kann_Spassky_Variation.md), B19) Variations.
- **Black** may instead delay the centre entirely (hypermodern tries — rare, but sound and genuinely offbeat):
- [**... d6**](./e4_openings/B07_Pirc_Defense.md) the Pirc Defense, forking into the [Austrian Attack](./e4_openings/B09_Pirc_Austrian_Attack.md) (4. f4) / [Classical](./e4_openings/B08_Pirc_Classical_System.md) (4. Nf3) / [150 Attack](./e4_openings/B07_Pirc_Defense.md#_Be3_) (4. Be3) after 2. d4 Nf6 3. Nc3 g6,
- [**... g6**](./e4_openings/B06_Modern_Defense.md) the Modern (Robatsch) Defense, the same idea reached by fianchettoing first, or
- [**... Nf6**](./e4_openings/B02_Alekhine_Defense.md) the Alekhine Defense, provoking White's centre forward instead of contesting it — leads to the [Four Pawns Attack](./e4_openings/B03_Alekhine_Defense.md#_f4_) if White pushes all the way, or to the [Modern Variation, Main Line](./e4_openings/B05_Alekhine_Modern_Bg4.md) (its own code, B05) after 3. d4 d6 4. Nf3 Bg4.

### From 1. e4 e5 ###
- **White** may develop its pieces with :
  - [**2. Nc3** The Vienna game](./e4_openings/C25_Vienna_Game.md) (C25-C29 fully swept) — forks to **2... Nc6**, live-tagged the *Max Lange Defence* (stays C25, forking the *Paulsen*/*Fyfe Gambit* sidelines and the *Vienna Gambit* proper, itself running through the *Steinitz Gambit* and a whole 19th-century romantic-gambit tail — the *Hamppe-Allgaier*, *Hamppe-Muzio*, and *Pierce Gambit* among them, none objectively rewarded per Stockfish); and **2... Nf6**, already live-tagged its own code, the [Falkbeer Variation (C26)](./e4_openings/C26_Vienna_Falkbeer_Variation.md) — itself forking the *Paulsen-Mieses*/*Mengarini* sidelines, the [Frankenstein-Dracula Variation (C27)](./e4_openings/C27_Vienna_Frankenstein_Dracula.md) (3. Bc4 Nxe4, one of the sharpest, most tactically dense tabiyas in the whole opening), the [Three Knights Variation (C28)](./e4_openings/C28_Vienna_Stanley_Three_Knights.md) (3. Bc4 Nc6), and the [Vienna Gambit (C29)](./e4_openings/C29_Vienna_Gambit.md) (3. f4 — the code attaches at this exact move, a genuine live-tagging surprise, running through the *Paulsen Attack* and the *Wurzburger trap* to close out the whole complex).
  - [**2. Bc4** The Bishop's Opening](./e4_openings/C23_Bishop_Opening.md) (C23-C24 fully swept) — forks to **2... Nf6**, already live-tagged its own code, the [Berlin Defense (C24)](./e4_openings/C24_Bishop_Opening_Berlin_Defense.md) (masters' overwhelming preference, 83.2%, converging with the Italian Game's own [Modern Bishop's Opening](./e4_openings/C24_Bishop_Opening_Berlin_Defense.md#_d3_) tabiya after 3. d3); **2... Bc5**, live-tagged the [Boi Variation](./e4_openings/C23_Bishop_Opening.md#_Bc5_) (a name divergence from `eco.md`'s own "Classical Variation" label), forking to nine further-named romantic-era gambits (the *Lopez*/*Lewis* Gambits, *Wing Gambit*, *MacDonnell double Gambit*, *Four Pawns' Gambit* among them) — several genuinely unsound per Stockfish despite the gambit label; and the real database rarities **2... c6** (the *Philidor Counterattack*, with its own *Lisitsyn Variation*) and **2... f5** (the *Calabrese Countergambit*, with its own *Jaenisch Variation*).
- **White** can play for tricks with :
  - [**2. Qh5** The Wayward Queen](./e4_openings/C20_KPG.md#_Qh5_)
- **White** may attack the undefended e5 pawn with :
  - [**2. f4** The King's Gambit](./e4_openings/C30_King_Gambit.md) (C30-C39 fully swept) — forks to the *Keene's Defence* (2... Qh4+), *Mafia Defence* (2... c5), *Norwalde Variation* (2...
