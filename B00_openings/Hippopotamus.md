# B00 Hippopotamus Defense <br> 1. e4 Nh6 #

An immediate knight move to the rim, developing toward g4 or f7/f5 rather than a central square — named after the broader "Hippopotamus" system (a wall of pawns on the 3rd rank behind fianchettoed bishops) that this move can transpose into, even though the immediate 1... Nh6 is a much more dubious, premature version of that idea. The knight is easily kicked or simply badly placed compared with developing toward the centre.
