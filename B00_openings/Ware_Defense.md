# B00 Ware Defense <br> 1. e4 a5 #

The mirror of [A00's own Ware Opening](https://github.com/onclemarcel/chess_flashcards/blob/main/A00_openings/Ware.md) (1. a4), attributed to the same 19th-century American player, Preston Ware. Grabs queenside space and prepares ... Ra6 to swing the rook across, but does nothing for development or the centre — genuinely inferior to a normal reply, though not catastrophically so.
