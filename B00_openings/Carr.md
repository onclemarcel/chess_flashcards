# B00 Carr Defense <br> 1. e4 h6 #

A rook-pawn move that does essentially nothing on move one beyond preparing a possible future ... g5, at the cost of a full tempo and no development at all. Even more passive than most of this family of irregularities, since it doesn't contest the centre or open a diagonal the way ... b6/... a6 do.
