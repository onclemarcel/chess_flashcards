# B00 Lemming Defense <br> 1. e4 Na6 #

Develops the queen's knight to the rim rather than its natural squares (c6 or d7), named for the lemming's folkloric reputation for pointless self-destructive behaviour. The knight aims for nothing useful on a6 and is easily kept out of the game — one of the most purposeless first replies in this whole family.
