# B00 Owen Defense <br> 1. e4 b6 #

Named after 19th-century English clergyman Reverend John Owen, who used it to beat several strong players of his day, including Joseph Blackburne. Fianchettoes the light-squared bishop to b7 immediately, aiming down the long diagonal at e4 and eventually g2, before deciding on a central structure — the same idea as the Queen's Indian, played a move earlier and against 1. e4 instead of 1. d4. The most-played of this whole family of first-move irregularities by a wide margin.
