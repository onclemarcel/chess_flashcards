# B00 Borg Defense <br> 1. e4 g5 #

The mirror image of [A00's own Grob Opening](https://github.com/onclemarcel/chess_flashcards/blob/main/A00_openings/Grob.md) (1. g4) — the name "Borg" is literally "Grob" spelled backwards. Weakens the kingside dark squares and the h4-e1 diagonal in front of Black's own king for no immediate gain, the same fundamental flaw as the Grob itself, just played a move later and by the side without the extra tempo.
