# B00 Barnes Defense <br> 1. e4 f6 #

Named, like [A00's own Barnes Opening](https://github.com/onclemarcel/chess_flashcards/blob/main/A00_openings/Barnes.md) (1. f3), after 19th-century English player Thomas Wilson Barnes. Weakens the a7-g1 diagonal and the e8-h5 diagonal toward Black's own king right away, for no gain in the centre or development — one of the more clearly unsound irregular replies to 1. e4.

> [!TIP]
> A genuine troll line rather than a serious defence: Black voluntarily walks the king to f7, giving up castling rights for no compensation at all. **Naming note**: this is a different "Fried Fox" from the one on [`A00_openings/Barnes.md`](https://github.com/onclemarcel/chess_flashcards/blob/main/A00_openings/Barnes.md#_mate_or_trap_) (White's 1. f3, nicknamed for the mate-in-1 trap it can walk into) — this one is live-tagged **B00 · Fried Fox Defense** by the explorer itself, a distinct piece of chess folklore that happens to share both the "Barnes" surname and the "Fried Fox" nickname purely by coincidence.
