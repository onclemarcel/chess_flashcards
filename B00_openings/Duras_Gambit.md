# B00 Duras Gambit <br> 1. e4 f5 #

Offers the f-pawn outright to fight for e4/the centre from the flank, in the same spirit as the Dutch Defense but a full tempo down and without a pawn already on d4 to justify it. Named for Czech grandmaster Oldřich Duras. Objectively one of the weaker gambits in this family — White simply takes the pawn and keeps a large advantage almost every time it's actually accepted.
