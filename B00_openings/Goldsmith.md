# B00 Goldsmith Defense <br> 1. e4 h5 #

Pushes the rook pawn two squares instead of one, gaining a little more kingside space than [Carr Defense](https://github.com/onclemarcel/chess_flashcards/blob/main/B00_openings/Carr.md) (1... h6) for the same lack of development or central contest. Occasionally used to pre-empt a future g4 advance from White, but otherwise a pure tempo loss.
