# B00 St. George Defense <br> 1. e4 a6 #

Best known for English GM Tony Miles's shock 1980 win over World Champion Anatoly Karpov with this exact move — a result that gave the line its patriotic English nickname (Saint George being England's patron saint). A genuine "do nothing yet" move: it doesn't stake any claim to the centre, but keeps every option open, including a later ... b5 to fianchetto the bishop or grab queenside space.
