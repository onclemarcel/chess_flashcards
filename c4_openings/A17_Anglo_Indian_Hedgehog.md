# A17 English Opening: Anglo-Indian Defense, Hedgehog System <br> 1. c4 Nf6 2. Nc3 e6 #

Spun off from [A16_Anglo_Indian_Queens_Knight.md](https://github.com/onclemarcel/chess_flashcards/blob/main/c4_openings/A16_Anglo_Indian_Queens_Knight.md), where **2... e6** used to live as a mention-only NOTE. Black keeps a small, elastic pawn chain rather than staking an immediate central claim — one of the English's most distinct strategic setups, waiting for White to overextend before striking back.
